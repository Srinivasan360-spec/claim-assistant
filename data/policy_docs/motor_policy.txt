Motor Secure Comprehensive - Policy Highlights (sample synthetic document)

Section 1. Cover overview. This is a comprehensive motor policy covering own damage to your vehicle and statutory third-party liability. Own damage cover applies to accident, theft, fire, flood and man-made calamities, subject to the policy terms.

Section 2. Insured Declared Value (IDV). The IDV is the market value of the vehicle reduced for depreciation. In a total loss or theft, settlement is limited to the IDV less the deductible. IDV is fixed at renewal and cannot exceed the ex-showroom price.

Section 3. Depreciation. Plastic and rubber parts depreciate at 50% per year, glass at 0%, and metal parts as per the age-based table in the policy. The claim is settled on the depreciated value of replaced parts, not the new part price.

Section 4. Deductible. A voluntary deductible of INR 1,000 applies per claim for own damage. A compulsory deductible applies as per the policy schedule for higher vehicle categories.

Section 5. Claim process for own damage. Intimate the claim within 48 hours of the incident. A surveyor inspects the vehicle and prepares a report. Repairs may begin at a network garage after approval, or you may claim reimbursement for repairs done elsewhere with the final invoice.

Section 6. Documents required. A copy of the FIR or police report where applicable, driving licence of the driver at the time of incident, vehicle registration certificate, repair estimate, and the claim form. For theft claims, an FIR and the vehicle keys are required.

Section 7. Third party liability. Damage or injury caused to other people or their property is covered under statutory third-party liability. Liability for third-party property damage is settled as per the surveyor and legal assessment, with no upper cap for bodily injury as per the Motor Vehicles Act.

Section 8. Exclusions. Driving without a valid licence, driving under the influence, use of the vehicle for hire without a commercial permit, and wear and tear or mechanical breakdown are excluded. Loss or damage arising before the policy inception date is not covered.

Section 9. Claim timelines. Intimation is acknowledged the same day. Survey is completed within 3 working days of intimation where access is available. Approved settlements are paid within 7 working days of final documents.

Section 10. Grievance redressal. If you disagree with a claim decision, file a grievance within 15 days of the decision letter quoting the claim ID. A written response is due within 15 days. You may also approach the insurance ombudsman for disputes below the prescribed monetary limit.
