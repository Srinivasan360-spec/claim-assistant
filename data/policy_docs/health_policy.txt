Family Health Optimizer - Policy Highlights (sample synthetic document)

Section 1. Cover overview. This policy reimburses reasonable and customary expenses for hospitalization, day-care procedures and pre/post-hospitalisation care, up to the sum insured in a policy year. Cashless treatment is available at network hospitals; at non-network hospitals you may claim reimbursement.

Section 2. Waiting periods. There is a 30-day waiting period from inception for all claims except accidental injuries. Pre-existing diseases are covered after 36 months of continuous coverage, subject to disclosure at purchase. Specific diseases have a 24-month waiting period as listed in the schedule.

Section 3. Room rent sub-limits. For a sum insured of INR 5,00,000 the room rent sub-limit is 2% of sum insured per day, that is INR 10,000 per day. Charges above the sub-limit are deducted proportionately from the settlement under the proportionate clause.

Section 4. Deductible and co-pay. A deductible of INR 5,000 applies per policy year on reimbursement claims. Where the policy schedule records a co-pay percentage, that percentage of the admissible claim is borne by the insured at settlement.

Section 5. Non-medical items. Items such as toiletries, cosmetics, telephone charges, entertainment equipment and non-prescribed supplements are not payable even if they appear on the hospital bill. A complete list of non-medical items is annexed to the policy schedule.

Section 6. Documents required. Intimate the claim within 24 hours of admission where possible. Required documents are the discharge summary, itemised bills, prescriptions, diagnostic reports, and the insurance card or policy number. For reimbursement claims, original bills must be submitted.

Section 7. Claim timelines. Fresh claims are acknowledged within 2 working days. Under review decisions are targeted within 3 working days of receiving complete documents. Approved settlements are paid within 5 working days. If documents are missing, the clock pauses until they are received.

Section 8. Exclusions. Cosmetic or refractive surgery without medical necessity, self-inflicted injury, war and nuclear perils, and treatment under the influence of intoxicants are excluded. Mental illness cover applies only where the policy schedule explicitly lists it.

Section 9. Pre and post hospitalisation. Expenses up to 30 days before and 60 days after a hospitalization are admissible when they relate to the same condition, subject to submission of bills within the claim window.

Section 10. Grievance redressal. If you disagree with a claim decision, file a grievance within 15 days of the decision letter with the claim ID and supporting evidence. A written response is due within 15 days of receipt. Escalation to the internal ombudsman is available after that.
