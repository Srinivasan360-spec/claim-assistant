Real-world health insurance practices in India - reference notes (synthetic demo, public information)

Section A. Who provides health insurance in India. Real-world health cover comes from companies such as Star Health and Allied Insurance (a health-only specialist), HDFC ERGO General Insurance, ICICI Lombard General Insurance, Care Health Insurance, ManipalCigna Life Insurance, Niva Bupa Health Insurance, Aditya Birla Health Insurance, Bajaj Allianz General Insurance and public-sector insurers such as New India Assurance and Oriental India. Their public websites (starhealth.in, hdfcergo.com, icicilombard.com, careinsurance.in, manipalcigna.com, nivabupa.com, bajajallianz.com, newindia.co.in) publish policy wordings, claim forms and hospital lists.

Section B. Familiar real products. Commonly seen plans include Star Family Health Optima, HDFC ERGO Optima Secure, ICICI Lombard iHealth, Bajaj Allianz Health Guard, Care Supreme and ManipalCigna ProHealth. Like our sample Family Health Optimizer, most real plans combine a sum insured with room-rent sub-limits, deductibles or co-pay options, a 30-day initial waiting period and a longer waiting period for pre-existing diseases.

Section C. Cashless versus reimbursement in practice. At a network hospital the insurer pays the hospital directly after pre-authorization, and the customer only pays their share; at a non-network hospital the customer pays first and claims reimbursement with original bills. Every insurer publishes a network of thousands of hospitals - always check the list before admission, because using a network hospital is the single biggest factor in a smooth cashless claim.

Section D. Regulatory time limits that real insurers follow. Under IRDAI health insurance regulations and master circulars (2024 and current), insurers must decide a cashless pre-authorization request within about 1 hour, approve final discharge within about 3 hours, and settle or reject a reimbursement claim within 30 days of receiving the last required document. A written grievance must be answered within 15 days, after which the customer may approach the Insurance Ombudsman. Verify the current wording on irdai.gov.in before quoting exact limits.

Section E. How real insurers are compared. Claim settlement ratio (CSR) is the share of claims an insurer settles; leading private general insurers such as HDFC ERGO, ICICI Lombard and Bajaj Allianz have publicly reported health CSR figures in the mid-to-high 90s percent in recent years, while Star Health figures vary by year and by whether cashless and reimbursement claims are counted together (public reports range from the high 80s to around 99 percent). Incurred claim ratio (ICR) is a second yardstick. Published numbers vary between sources and years - check the insurer's latest annual report or IRDAI's public disclosures for the current figure.

Section F. Reading a real decision letter. Real decision letters list every deduction: room-rent sub-limit and the proportionate clause, deductible, co-pay, non-medical items and consumables. If a deduction looks wrong the customer can ask for a breakup review within the policy's grievance window, which is usually 15 days from the decision letter.

Section G. Why this matters for this demo. The synthetic claims in this project were shaped to match these real practices, so an explanation that reads correctly here reads correctly against a real insurer's process too.
